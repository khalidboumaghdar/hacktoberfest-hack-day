# 1337 Coding School

## Overview

1337 Coding School is a Moroccan school of digital science focused on computer science and computer programming. It is part of the University Mohammed VI Polytechnic (UM6P) ecosystem and is governed by the OCP Group. The school was founded in 2018 in Khouribga, Morocco.

1337 uses a project-based and peer-to-peer learning model inspired by the French school 42 and the international 42 Network. Its approach emphasizes self-directed learning, collaboration, autonomy, practical work, and problem-solving rather than traditional lectures.

According to the source article, tuition is free and admission does not require a particular academic diploma. Applicants are selected through tests and an intensive immersion phase known as the “pool” or “piscine.”

- **Founded:** 2018
- **Founder / governing organization:** OCP Group
- **Parent institution:** University Mohammed VI Polytechnic (UM6P)
- **Director named in the source:** Larbi El Hilali
- **Country:** Morocco
- **Website:** https://1337.ma/

## History and campuses

- **2018 — Khouribga:** 1337 was launched and its first campus opened in Khouribga, in a site described by the source as having been rehabilitated from local industrial heritage.
- **2019 — Benguerir:** A second campus opened in Benguerir within the UM6P ecosystem to increase capacity.
- **2019 — 42 Network:** 1337 joined the international 42 Network.
- **2022 — Tetouan:** A campus named 1337 Med opened in the Tetouan Shore zone, in partnership with the Tanger Med Foundation and UM6P.
- **2024 — Rabat:** A fourth campus opened in Rabat within the UM6P ecosystem to increase capacity.
- **Campuses listed in the source:** Khouribga, Benguerir, Tetouan, and Rabat.

Campus details and the list of locations may change over time; check the official 1337 website for current information.

## Meaning of the name

The name “1337” comes from “leet” or “leetspeak,” an internet and hacker-culture slang term derived from “elite.” In leetspeak, numbers are used in place of visually similar letters. The source says the name reflects the school's connection to programming and hacker culture.

## Organization and pedagogy

1337's educational model is inspired by 42. It emphasizes:

- Self-learning and independent problem-solving.
- Peer-to-peer learning and collaboration.
- Project-based work.
- Practical programming and computer-science tasks.
- Learning without conventional lectures or a traditional academic structure.

According to the source article, admission does not depend on formal academic qualifications. Applicants take selection tests and, if selected to continue, an intensive immersion period called the “pool” or “piscine.” During this period, their learning ability and problem-solving skills are evaluated.

## Curriculum

The source describes three main curriculum phases: the common core, an internship, and the advanced curriculum.

### Common core

- The common core generally lasts around 1.5 to 2 years.
- Students work on foundational projects and evaluations.
- Topics include programming, algorithms, computer science, systems administration, software engineering, and collaborative problem-solving.
- The phase includes examinations.

### Internship

After completing the common core, students undertake a mandatory internship described as lasting four to six months. The internship provides professional experience before the advanced curriculum.

### Advanced curriculum

The advanced curriculum may last from 1 to 4.5 additional years, depending on a student's pace and chosen specializations.

Possible specialization areas listed in the source include:

- Artificial intelligence
- Web development
- Game development
- Cybersecurity
- DevOps
- Operating-system and kernel development
- Compiler design
- Cryptography
- Mathematics-related computing disciplines

The curriculum is based on project-based and peer-to-peer learning inspired by the 42 Network. Durations and requirements should be checked against current official school information.

## Student life and services

The source article reports that 1337 provides free accommodation and meal services at its campuses, subject to eligibility and academic progression or participation requirements.

- At Khouribga and Rabat, students may access dormitory facilities affiliated with UM6P.
- At Tetouan and Benguerir, eligible students may receive financial assistance for accommodation.
- Meals are described as including breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
- Students at Benguerir, Khouribga, and Rabat may access sports facilities on UM6P campuses.
- Student-run clubs and societies cover areas such as robotics, cybersecurity, competitive programming, entrepreneurship, design, music, and sports.

These service details may vary by campus and over time. They are claims from the source article, not independently verified current guarantees.

## Competitions and student achievements

The source article reports the following achievements and activities:

- Since 2023, 1337 has regularly ranked among leading institutions in CodinGame programming challenges.
- In March 2026, the school reportedly placed first worldwide in the CodinGame Winter Challenge.
- In 2025, 1337 reportedly won its first Moroccan National Programming Contest (MNPC) championship.
- In 2024, a team from 1337 reportedly placed second in a national cybersecurity competition involving Moroccan higher-education and engineering institutions.
- The source says 1337 teams were the highest-ranked in Morocco on CTFtime for three consecutive years, from 2023 to 2025.
- In 2026, a team representing 1337 reportedly placed second in the Morocco Academia Cyber Competition (MACC), described in the source as involving 1,156 teams from more than 150 higher-education institutions and engineering schools.
- The Khouribga campus organizes CyberOdyssey, a Capture The Flag (CTF) cybersecurity competition. The source describes it as a major national event, bringing together around 20 leading teams and offering prizes exceeding US$21,000.
- In 2023, students from 1337 participated in Reddit's collaborative r/place event. The source credits students with creating a Moroccan flag and map on the final canvas as part of Morocco's collective participation.

Competition results and event details are time-sensitive; verify them with the linked source or the event organizer before treating them as current.

## Educational positioning and admissions philosophy

The source characterizes 1337 as an alternative to conventional higher education in Morocco. Its admissions philosophy emphasizes aptitude and potential rather than formal diplomas or social background. Candidates are described as being evaluated through logic, memory, and other selection tests.

The source also notes that France 24 described the school as “Morocco's Silicon Valley.” This is a media characterization, not an official institutional title.

## Progression and certification

The source describes a level-based progression system. The certificate a student receives depends on the level reached when leaving the program.

Examples of internal certificates listed in the source:

- **Level 10:** Junior Developer
- **Level 13:** Developer
- **Level 16:** Advanced Developer
- **Level 19:** Computer Scientist
- **Level 21:** Computer Architect

The source says students who complete the common core without continuing into specialization may leave at levels generally ranging from 10 to 12. Students who continue in the advanced curriculum may reach higher levels depending on their progression and specialization.

In addition to internal school certificates, the source says students who satisfy specific academic and administrative conditions may be eligible for French professional certification titles delivered through the 42 Network:

- **Concepteur développeur de solutions informatiques** — RNCP / EQF level 6; the source says this may be accessible from level 16 under additional conditions.
- **Expert en architecture informatique** — RNCP / EQF level 7; the source says this may be accessible from level 21 under additional conditions.

These titles and eligibility conditions should be confirmed with 1337 or the relevant certification authority. Reaching a stated level alone should not be assumed to guarantee an RNCP title.

## Relationship with UM6P, OCP Group, and the 42 Network

- **UM6P:** The source identifies 1337 as part of the UM6P ecosystem and describes links to campus facilities and student services.
- **OCP Group:** Identified as the school's founder and governing organization.
- **42 Network:** 1337 joined the international network in 2019. Its pedagogy is inspired by the 42 model, including peer learning and project-based education.
- **Tanger Med Foundation:** The source identifies it as a partner in the opening of the 1337 Med campus in Tetouan.
