## 42 Wolfsburg Wins First Place at the KI Hackathon

Can AI transform public services, optimize city operations, and improve urban living?

This question drove the KI Hackathon Wolfsburg, where developers, designers, and innovators came together to tackle real-world challenges. Organized by [WolfsburgDigital](https://living-in-wolfsburg.de/wolfsburg-in-germany/this-is-wolfsburg/wolfsburgdigital/), alongside [Lufthansa Industry Solutions](https://www.lufthansa-industry-solutions.com/de-en/), [Stadt Wolfsburg](https://www.wolfsburg.de/), [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/de-de/microsoft-supports-programming-school-42-wolfsburg/), Volkswagen, and T-Systems International, the event brought together 14 teams and over 70 participants, all striving to develop impactful AI-driven solutions.

Among fierce competition, [42 Wolfsburg’s](https://42wolfsburg.de/) team, TALEMT, claimed first place with their AI-powered solution for optimizing document processing in Immigration Offices.

Competing against industry professionals and seasoned developers, their success highlights the strength of 42’s peer-driven learning model and its ability to foster innovation.
