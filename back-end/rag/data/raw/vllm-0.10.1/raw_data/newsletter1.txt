42 GLOBAL SEMINAR
1337 at the 42 Global Seminar in Tirana
At the end of September, we took the 1337 Morocco experience to the fourth 42 Global Seminar, hosted inside the Pyramid of Tirana.

For five days, we joined teams from across the 42 Network to exchange ideas, challenge our practices, and explore what the future of peer-led education can look like. We brought the perspective of a community growing across Khouribga, Benguerir, Rabat, and Tetouan.

We shared how our campuses support autonomy, build active student communities, and turn peer learning into a daily experience. Just as importantly, we listened, learned, and discovered new approaches from campuses facing their own challenges around the world.

We returned from Tirana with stronger connections, fresh ideas, and new possibilities for collaboration. Every conversation added something we can bring back to our campuses—and every exchange gave us another reason to keep building together.

1337 Alumni Platform

It’s finally here: the 1337 Alumni Platform is live.
After months of preparation and building, the platform is ready — and the most exciting part starts now: bringing the community to life.

Alumni are already joining, completing their profiles, reconnecting with former classmates, sharing where they are today, and discovering what other members of the 1337 community are building. Every connection, comment, message, and shared opportunity helps make the network more active and valuable.

FOR ALUMNI
Sign in with your Alumni account to access the full network: profiles, connections, communities, events, conversations, and the wider alumni experience. This part of the platform is reserved for alumni.

FOR STUDENTS

If you are an alumnus, complete your profile, explore the platform, reconnect with someone you have not seen in years, and help activate the community. Know an alumnus who has not joined yet? Invite them — the more of the community we bring together, the more useful the network becomes.

BENGUERIR CAMPUS
WEB DEV CLUB
1337 Web Club Opens Its Activity Planning to the Community
Alongside the larger projects and initiatives already planned by the club, the 1337 Web Club is involving the wider student community in shaping part of its upcoming learning content.

Through a dedicated form, students can express their interests, suggest topics for future sessions, and indicate whether they would like to contribute as organizers or mentors.

The objective is to complement the club’s main projects with targeted activities that reflect students’ current needs while creating more opportunities to contribute to the club’s learning dynamic.

Weekly Women’s Spiritual Gathering
The Siraj Club organizes a weekly gathering for female students, offering a calm space for reflection, discussion, and spiritual learning.

Each session explores inspiring stories from the Companions and other notable figures from early Islamic history, highlighting resilience, courage, generosity, sincerity, and faith.

Participants reflect on these stories, exchange perspectives, and draw meaningful lessons for personal and everyday life.

LeetMusic & Friends Opened the Academic Year with a Collaborative Jam
For its first edition of the academic year, LeetMusic & Friends teamed up with the Architecture Students Association for an evening of live music and creative exchange at the SAP+D Cafeteria.

Spontaneous jam sessions, live improvisation, vocals, and DJ sets invited students to move freely between performing and enjoying the atmosphere.

The collaboration connected musicians from different communities and used music to bring students together beyond their usual campus circles.

Two Game Nights, One Wider Community
On 11 September, LeetEvents brought students together at the Agora for PS5 games, board games, and a relaxed evening beyond the academic routine.

On 26 September, the format returned with GameDev and opened its doors to the wider UM6P community, helping students meet beyond their usual circles through games and interactive group activities.

Results worth celebrating
Achievements | Hall of Fame

Iconics Reign Again: La Liga 5.0 Champions After a 4–3 Thriller
Iconics lifted the La Liga 5.0 trophy after a thrilling 4–3 victory over Lmountakhab in a closely contested final.

Established as one of 1337’s most successful football teams since 2022, Iconics added another title after several weeks of competition. Lmountakhab pushed them all the way in an intense seven-goal final, bringing the fifth edition of the student-led tournament to a memorable close.

1337 Continues Its Global Podium Streak at the CodinGame Summer Challenge 2026
1337 Coding School secured 2nd place worldwide in the school ranking, ahead of 42 Paris after a tightly contested competition.

This edition emphasized AI-driven bot programming, challenging participants to build autonomous agents able to analyze a dynamic game environment, make strategic decisions, and continuously adapt against competing bots.

The result extends 1337’s strong international trajectory and highlights the consistency, technical level, and competitive mindset of its student community.

Student Founders

From 1337 to the future of agriculture
This month, we put the spotlight on INBAT, an AgriTech startup founded within our 1337 community and led by Ahmed Billa. In our new video, we showcase the team, the problem they chose to solve, and the ambition driving their journey.

INBAT brings livestock tracking, milk-production monitoring, expense management, and AI-powered analysis into one smart farm-management platform. We are proud to share how members of our community are using technology to help farmers save time, reduce losses, and make better decisions.
