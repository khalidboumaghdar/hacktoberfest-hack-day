NASA Recognition for Space4Everyone
 

The Space4Everyone Club at 1337 has been selected for the NASA Astro Camp Community Partners Program (ACCP) 2026.

 

This recognition will allow the club to locally develop and run a Space Camp-inspired educational initiative, while selected mentors will receive official NASA training, certifications, and access to exclusive educational resources.

A major recognition for a student-led initiative — and a strong signal of the growing impact of the 1337 communitybeyond campus.

Project Spotlight — AI Assistant by the Robotics Club
 

During UM6P Science Week 2026, the Robotics Club developed a voice-enabled AI assistant powered by Raspberry Pi.

 

Designed to interact naturally through voice, automate tasks, control connected devices, and provide real-time information, the project combines AI, IoT, and user experience into one working system.

 

More than a prototype, it’s a clear example of convergence in action.
