# Code Your Future,

## For Free

UM6P is home to 1337, Morocco’s first tuition-free, open-access coding school, available 24/7 to anyone, regardless of background. It offers full immersion in tech through hands-on, engaging learning in an atmosphere where curiosity and code come together to shape the future.

# Dive into coding world

Join 1337 and discover a revolutionary way to learn programming - no classes, no teachers, just hands-on innovation. Whether you're a beginner or a self-starter, your journey into technology starts here, free of charge.

[Visit the website](https://1337.ma 'Visit the website') [Check our channel](https://www.youtube.com/@1337FutureIsLoading 'Check our channel')

Based on peer-to-peer learning and project-based progress, 1337 unleashes student creativity. It breaks traditional boundaries of education to make coding fun, accessible, and collaborative. Inspired by 42 Paris, it follows the world’s top-ranked tech training model.
