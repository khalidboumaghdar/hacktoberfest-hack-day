Opening Doors to Future Builders

 

Throughout April and May, 1337 Khouribga welcomed students from schools and universities across the region, offering campus tours, workshops, and an introduction to the 1337 learning model.

 

Student Session — TIG

 

A student-led session brought peers together around technical learning and knowledge sharing, reflecting the collaborative spirit at the heart of the 1337 pedagogy.

HackAI 2026 
 

More than 90 participants from 21 schools and universities gathered at 1337 Benguerir for four days of learning, innovation, and collaboration around artificial intelligence.

 

Annual Swimming Competition

 

More than 50 students participated in the annual swimming competition, celebrating progress, perseverance, and healthy competition across multiple disciplines.

Abdallah Guennoun High School Visit

 

1337 MED welcomed students from Abdallah Guennoun High School for an immersive visit introducing them to coding, peer learning, and the opportunities offered by the 1337 ecosystem.

LEETCOMPETE at JNJD Programming Contest
 

Two LEETCOMPETE teams represented the campus at the JNJD Programming Contest, securing 10th and 25th place and continuing to strengthen the competitive programming culture at 1337.

The 1337 Khouribga team took a well-deserved break with a retreat to Cathédrale Imsfrane, combining adventure, team building, and shared moments outside the campus.

From rafting and quad biking to reaching the summit, the experience was a reminder that strong communities are built not only through work, but also through the moments shared together.

HackAI 2026 — AI for the Rural World
Organized by the 1337AI Club, HackAI 2026 brought together 90+ participants from 21 schools and universities to build AI solutions addressing real challenges faced by rural communities. 

1337AI Launches Its New Community Platform 
 

The 1337AI Club launched its new online platform, bringing together the club’s projects, hackathons, workshops, and community initiatives in one place.


SysOps Returns
 

SysOps is making its return, bringing together students interested in DevOps, cloud computing, automation, Linux systems, and infrastructure engineering through workshops, projects, and hands-on learning.


MAP Game Dev Opens Onboarding
 

The MAP Game Development Club (Rabat) has opened onboarding for new members, offering a structured learning path in game development through workshops, collaborative projects, and hands-on experience with Godot Engine.

Club Fair & Community Discovery
 

Organized by Leet Horizon, the Club Fair gave students the opportunity to discover campus clubs, connect with members, and explore new communities, initiatives, and projects.

NET-LEARN by NETWEAVER
 

NETWEAVER (Rabat) launched NET-LEARN, a platform built by students to help the community learn modern web development through curated resources, technical content, and community-driven initiatives.

13SQL — Built by Praxis
 

13SQL is a database engine MVP developed by Praxis in Rabat, showcasing collaborative engineering, backend systems design, code review practices, and project planning.

The project reflects the club’s ambition to build complex technical systems from the ground up while strengthening engineering skills through real-world development.


Girls’ Quran Recitation Celebration
 

1337 Khouribga organized a ceremony recognizing students who excelled in Quran recitation, creating a meaningful moment of celebration and community.

Shoutout to LEETCOMPETE
 

A special congratulations to LEETCOMPETE for proudly representing the campus at the JNJD Programming Contest and continuing to grow the competitive programming culture across the community.
