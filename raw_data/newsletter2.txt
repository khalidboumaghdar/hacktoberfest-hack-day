TOP STORY
The Piscine Experience: Powered by the Community
The 2026 Piscine season has officially come to an end. Across our campuses, a new generation of poolers completed one of the most demanding and rewarding experiences of their journey. From validation days and first projects to Rushes, final exams, and late-night debugging sessions, they discovered what it truly means to learn through collaboration, resilience, and peer support.

This journey would not have been possible without the incredible commitment of our students, staff, volunteers, and clubs, who welcomed newcomers, organized activities, answered questions, and created an environment where everyone could grow together.

To everyone who made this year's Piscine unforgettable—thank you for making it more than a challenge. Thank you for building a community.

La Liga 5.0 – Grand Final
After eight weeks of competition organized by the Athleets Club, Iconics and Lmontakhab secured their place in the grand final, closing another successful edition of one of the campus's longest-running traditions.

ACROSS OUR CAMPUSES

The Piscine Comes to a Close
The second Piscine concluded after weeks of coding, collaboration, Rush projects, and final evaluations. The journey began with a dedicated staff meet-and-greet, giving poolers the opportunity to connect with the campus team before diving into the challenge.

Students Teaching Students
Poolers embraced the 1337 spirit by organizing peer-learning sessions covering AI during the Piscine, Makefiles, headers, pointers, and C fundamentals, proving that everyone can be both a learner and a teacher.

1337 Summer Competition
The first edition of the 1337 Summer Competition continued throughout the summer with football, basketball, volleyball, handball, and padel tournaments, bringing students and staff together through teamwork and friendly competition.

Supporting Poolers Beyond Code
After each major Piscine milestone, staff organized exchange sessions where poolers could share feedback, ask questions, and receive encouragement before enjoying community Game Nights.

Game Night with a Live DJ
To celebrate the final stretch of the Piscine, LeetsEvents organized a special Game Night featuring a live DJ, creating a memorable evening where poolers could relax and celebrate together.

Behind Every Successful Piscine Is an Incredible Team

Siraj Welcomes Poolers
The Siraj Club welcomed the new cohort with encouraging messages and small treats, helping poolers feel at home from their very first days on campus.

Throughout the Piscine, CataLeet organized two community activities every week, including movie nights, board games, and social gatherings, giving poolers a chance to unwind, connect, and recharge.

Built by students for students, Collox helps poolers and 1337 students find accommodation and roommates before the Piscine starts, making it easier to settle in and join the community from day one. The Collox team presented the project across all 1337 campuses.

STUDENT ADVENTURES

One of our students completed an incredible solo bikepacking journey from Tangier to Marrakech, covering 1,032 km in 10 days. A remarkable personal challenge that reflects the perseverance, autonomy, and adventurous spirit that define the 1337 community.
