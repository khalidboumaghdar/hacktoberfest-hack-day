SPORTTIMES — No More Ghost Matches

📍 Built by Mohamed El Hadj

Football matches often disappear inside crowded group chats—players miss updates, confirmations remain unclear, and games fall apart.

SPORTTIMES is a mobile app designed to make sports organization visual and social. Players can discover matches, view formations and participants, join communities, manage lineups, and organize tournaments in one place.

The product is currently in development, with 1337 planned as its first campus launch.
